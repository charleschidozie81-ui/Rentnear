How RentNear works

RentNear helps you find agents and suppliers near you. Pick an area, then call or WhatsApp them directly.

How we verify

Before an agent or supplier gets the Verified by us tick, we:

  1. Call them and confirm their name, phone number and what they offer.
  2. Meet them in person.

Providers without the tick have not finished these checks yet.

The tick does not guarantee a house, an item or a price. Always check the property or item yourself before you pay.

Stay safe

  • Meet the agent and see the property before you pay anything.
  • Ask for a receipt and a written agreement.
  • Ask what every fee is for, including the viewing fee.
  • Report anything that looks wrong, so we can remove the listing.

Your privacy

RentNear does not ask you to create an account or give personal details.

When you tap Call or WhatsApp, your phone opens a call or chat with that provider. What you share with them is between you and them.

We show a provider's phone number only with their permission, and we remove it when they ask.

Our web host may keep basic technical records, as most websites do. If this changes, we will say so here.

Terms of use

RentNear is a directory. We connect renters with agents and suppliers, but we are not part of any rental, payment or agreement.

Prices, fees, deposits and conditions are agreed directly between you and the provider.

We try to keep our information correct, but it can change.
